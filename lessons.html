iii.

Questions

What should I bring and wear?

Bring a pillow and blanket for comfort. Purnima will confirm anything else when you book.

What does a sound bath feel like?

At most sound baths you lie down and let the sound wash over you. Some people drift towards sleep, some simply rest. There is no right way to do it.

Is it safe in pregnancy, with a pacemaker, or with epilepsy?

Please check with your doctor first, and tell Purnima beforehand so she can plan the session with you.

Sound healing is complementary care, not a replacement for medical treatment.
